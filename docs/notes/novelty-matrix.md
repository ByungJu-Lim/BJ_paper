## Claim-by-claim novelty

| Claim | Closest Prior Work | Key Difference |
|---|---|---|
| C0 — Embedded correlation is a published fouling correlation (Ebert-Panchal, `@wilson2017twenty`), chosen independently of the simulator, and the simulator adds a mechanism the correlation omits (induction period, `@yang2020computational`), so the residual has real work. | `gallup2023physics` | Gallup et al. embed a first-principles CSTR ODE in the hybrid but do not establish that the simulator adds a mechanism the embedded model omits via a separately sourced citation; the embedded model is a domain-specific CSTR equation, not a published fouling correlation (Ebert-Panchal, `@wilson2017twenty`) whose omissions are independently documented (`@yang2020computational`). The latter two sources supply components—a correlation and an omitted mechanism—but do not construct or compare a hybrid, so `gallup2023physics` remains the closest structural precedent. C0 is still an assumed design requirement: the Approach must select and document a simulator that actually implements at least one independently sourced omitted mechanism before the experiment can test the residual's workload via the noise-floor falsifier. |
| C1 — Black-box median extrapolation RMSE (union-rule outer-20% tail) ≥ 1.5× its median interpolation RMSE across 10 repetitions. | `gallup2023physics` | Gallup et al. test a black-box baseline outside its training window (400–420 °C → 430–450 °C) but report a single trial's percent error, not a median extrapolation/interpolation RMSE ratio across 10 repetitions; no repeated-seed design, no union-rule split, and on CSTR temperature, not shell-and-tube fouling resistance. Although `he2025machine` and `hosseini2022novel` are closer in application domain, neither reports an extrapolation split, so `gallup2023physics` is closest on the extrapolation design that defines C1. |
| C2 — Hybrid (parameter-matched within 10%, identical search budget) achieves median extrap RMSE ≥ 10% lower than black-box, lower in ≥ 8/10 reps (paired Wilcoxon p < 0.05). Contingent on C0. | `gallup2023physics` | Gallup et al. compare an additive-residual hybrid against a black-box under extrapolation, but the hybrid's capacity is unmatched (5-node hidden layers vs the baseline's 6 layers of 20), the result is a single trial with no paired statistical test, and the hybrid generalized only slightly better than the baseline. Its competing loss-penalty PINN generalized substantially better than the residual hybrid (2.28% vs 4.50% out-of-range error), making the precedent partly negative for the architecture tested here. This paper does not erase that result; it tests whether the residual-hybrid comparison survives trainable-parameter matching within 10%, 10 repetitions, and a paired Wilcoxon signed-rank test on shell-and-tube fouling resistance. |
| C3 — Hybrid median interpolation RMSE does not exceed black-box by more than 10% relative. Contingent on C0. | `gallup2023physics` | Gallup et al. report in-range percent errors for both models (hybrid 0.80%, baseline 0.85%) but as a single trial without matched capacity, no repeated-seed median, and no relative-tolerance threshold. This paper tests median interpolation RMSE parity within 10% relative under matched capacity across 10 repetitions on shell-and-tube fouling resistance. |
