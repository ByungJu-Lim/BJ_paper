This manuscript reports a controlled synthetic benchmark comparing a
capacity-matched, purely data-driven surrogate against an additive-residual
physics-informed hybrid for shell-and-tube fouling-resistance prediction,
with an explicit interpolation/extrapolation design. The one directly
relevant precedent (Gallup et al., 2023) left its hybrid-versus-black-box
comparison confounded by an unmatched trainable-parameter count and reported
no paired statistical test; this paper controls capacity exactly and applies
a paired, preregistered one-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank test across 10
repetitions. We confirm the benchmark poses a genuine extrapolation
challenge for the black-box model (extrapolation RMSE approximately 2.9x
interpolation RMSE), but our preregistered transcription/unit/input-mapping
alarm suspended adjudication of whether the embedded correlation and the
simulator are independent enough for the hybrid's residual term to have
real work to do, because the correlation predicted near-zero fouling across
the training envelope in this run. We report that finding, and the resulting
hybrid-versus-black-box comparison, as data rather than as a settled verdict
on the additive-residual hypothesis, and discuss what a follow-up design
would need to change to adjudicate it.
